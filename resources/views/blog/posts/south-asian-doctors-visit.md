We were honored to host a delegation of esteemed doctors from across South Asia who visited our Integrated Centre for Geriatric and Palliative Care. The visit was a significant step towards fostering global collaboration in the field of elderly care and provided an invaluable opportunity for the exchange of knowledge and best practices.

The delegation, which included specialists in geriatrics, palliative medicine, and public health, was given a comprehensive tour of our facilities. They had the opportunity to interact with our medical team, caregivers, and residents, gaining firsthand insight into our unique model of care. The doctors were particularly impressed by our holistic approach, which combines medical treatment with emotional, social, and spiritual support.

During their visit, the delegation participated in a series of workshops and discussions focused on the challenges and opportunities in geriatric care in the South Asian region. They shared their experiences and insights, and we explored potential avenues for collaboration in research, training, and policy advocacy.

We are confident that this visit will pave the way for a stronger partnership between our nations in our shared mission to improve the quality of life for the elderly. We look forward to continuing this dialogue and working together to create a more age-friendly world.
